Parking for the Cary Arts Center

Sunday, October 4, 2026 · matinee at 3:00 PM

  • 101 Dry Avenue, Cary, NC
  • Curtain 3:00 PM, bows end 5:45 PM
  • Cast and crew arrive in the early afternoon
  • Soccer on the school field 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM
  • Compiled September 28, 2026

In short

Parking will be tight that afternoon. A youth soccer tournament on the school field next door runs until 3:00 PM and fills the school lots. Across Dry Avenue, the park has a free youth theater show and a dog training class from 2:00 PM, just as patrons arrive. The library is open from 1:00 PM and holds some of the deck's spaces for its patrons. As the matinee lets out, people are arriving for a free 6:30 PM concert in the park. The arts center's own lot holds 24 cars for a 395-seat theater, and Dry Avenue in front of it has no parking.

Where to park

Cast and crew

Arriving in the early afternoon, at the tournament's peak

  1. Park in the Walnut Street Parking Deck (D1): 680 ft to the loading dock, about 3 minutes' walk.
  2. If it is full or closed, use the Old Cary Library Park Lot (L6): 810 ft to the dock, about 3 minutes' walk, 55 spaces, 2 of them accessible.
  3. If both are full, use the Academy Street Parking Deck (D2): 1575 ft to the dock, about 6 minutes' walk.
  4. The school lots (L2 and L4) and Faculty Avenue (S1, S2) are open to the public but likely to be full.

Patrons

Arriving 2:00 to 3:00 PM, leaving about 6:00 PM

  1. Park in the Walnut Street Parking Deck (D1): 570 ft to the front door, about 2 minutes' walk. The gravel lot in front of it (L7) is public too.
  2. If it is full or closed, use the Old Cary Library Park Lot (L6): 605 ft, about 2 minutes' walk, 55 spaces, 2 of them accessible.
  3. If both are full, use the Academy Street Parking Deck (D2): 1370 ft, about 5 minutes' walk, 225 public spaces.
  4. The school lots (L2 and L4) are free and open to the public on a Sunday, but the tournament will have filled them.
  5. There is no street parking on Dry Avenue, S Harrison Avenue, Kildaire Farm Road or Walnut Street, so do not circle there looking for a space.

Accessible parking and drop-off

All afternoon

  1. Arts center lot (L1), entered from Dry Avenue: 4 accessible spaces, first come. 125 ft to the front door by the ramp.
  2. Drop-off is inside that lot (L1), beside the stairs and ramp. The Town does not allow stopping at the front curb.
  3. The Walnut Street Parking Deck (D1) has 20 accessible spaces.
  4. The Academy Street Parking Deck (D2) has 7 accessible spaces on its public levels.
